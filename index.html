Context Collapse

The internet has always produced stories. Today, it produces worlds, conspiracies, fandoms, archives, anonymous identities, platform myths, AI hallucinations, and collective fictions. What stories are we telling? Which stories survive the internet?

Sixteen vertical timelines. Posts are speech bubbles of emotive colour. A timeline reacts by quoting a bubble from beside it, redrawing it inside a frame of its own reaction colour. Quotes of quotes nest, and past three rings the innermost surviving reaction becomes the fill — the original colour is gone.

One engine, one config per page. Every preview below is the piece itself, held on a single frame, at seed c0ffee.

Palettes

Same rules, different colour. The palette is a config; the mechanics are not.

Fills

The fill vocabulary is lifted from 10K Drop: a soft gradient, and repeating cells holding a spot, a check or a stripe. The second colour is always the post's own shifted in value, so a pattern is a texture within one voice rather than a second voice smuggled in.

space pause   r reseed   s save a still. The seed lives in the URL fragment, so any frame is reproducible: append #c0ffee to open that one again. Append ?still to hold the first frame, as these previews do.
